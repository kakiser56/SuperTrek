By using a Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information Collection And Use

Any information a Service collects is stored on your device only. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

### Daily Tracker for Weight Loss

**Personal Data.** While using this Service, we may ask you to provide certain personally identifiable information, such as your name. It is stored on your device and is never transmitted to us.

**Usage Data.** If the app crashes, your device may collect diagnostic data such as the type of device and operating system version. This information relates to crashes only and is handled by Apple's standard crash reporting, subject to your device's own settings.

### Super Trek

Super Trek collects no personal data of any kind. It has no network access. Your game in progress and your sound and haptics preferences are saved on your device only and never leave it. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party code.

## Tracking & Cookies Data

We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies in any Service.

## Use, Transfer, and Disclosure Of Data

Our Services send no data back to us. Your information is never transmitted to us or to any other service, and we do not disclose your data to anyone.

## Children's Privacy

Our Services do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. Because our Services store data only on the device and transmit nothing to us, we hold no data about any user. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has entered personal information into a Service, deleting the app removes that information.
